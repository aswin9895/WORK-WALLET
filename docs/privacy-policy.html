Privacy Policy

Work & Wallet

Android app by Aswin Saseendran · Effective date: 13 November 2026

In short: Work & Wallet works fully offline. Everything you enter is stored only on your own phone. The app has no accounts, no ads and no analytics, and it never sends your data to us or to anyone else. A file leaves your phone only when you choose to save or share it.

1. Who we are

Work & Wallet ("the app") is developed by Aswin Saseendran. It helps you record catering work and payments to workers, and keep track of your own bank money (salary months, expenses, savings and money you will get back). This policy explains what happens to the information you put into the app.

2. Information you enter

You decide what to type into the app. This can include:

  • names and phone numbers of catering companies and of people you work with;
  • work records: dates, places, sessions, amounts, extras and payments;
  • bank account names you choose (for example "HDFC") and balances, salary, expenses, income, savings and money lent or repaid, with your notes.

The app does not ask for bank account numbers, card numbers, passwords, your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.

3. Where your data is stored

  • All data is stored in a database inside the app's private storage on your phone. Other apps cannot read it.
  • We do not run any server for this app, and the app has no sign-in. We never receive, see or store your data.
  • Uninstalling the app or using "Clear storage" in Android settings deletes all of its data from your phone.

4. What the app does not do

  • No data collection, tracking or analytics.
  • No advertising and no advertising IDs.
  • No selling or sharing of personal data with anyone.
  • No third-party services that receive your data.

5. Files you choose to save or share

Some features create a file only when you ask for it:

  • Reports (PDF or image) of your catering work or money.
  • Backup: one file containing all your app data, so you can restore it later or on another phone.

If you tap Save, the file is saved to your phone's Downloads or Pictures folder. If you tap Share, Android's share menu opens and the file goes only to the app or person you pick (for example WhatsApp, Google Drive or email). Once shared, that service's own privacy policy applies. Restore only reads the backup file you choose with Android's file picker.

Backup files contain your data in readable form, so keep them somewhere safe.

6. Permissions

  • Storage (only on Android 9 and older): to save reports and backups to Downloads / Pictures when you tap Save. Newer Android versions need no storage permission for this.
  • Internet / network state: these are declared by standard software components the app is built with (the framework and its file-handling library). The app itself does not use the internet and sends none of your data anywhere.

7. Children

The app is a work and money record-keeping tool for adults and is not directed to children under 13. It does not knowingly collect any information from anyone.

8. Your choices and control

  • You can edit or delete any record in the app at any time.
  • You can delete everything by uninstalling the app or clearing its storage.
  • Backup and report files you saved or shared are under your control and can be deleted from wherever you keep them.

9. Future changes

If a future version adds features that change how data is handled (for example an optional online sync), this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the effective date above will change.
